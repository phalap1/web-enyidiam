Mgbeke Kalu Okoro is a daughter born to the mgbocha family, to Kalu Okoro(father) and Abenzi Kalu(mother formerly married from isuochi to Arochukwu) Mama was seventy-eight years at the time of here passing. Mgbeke kalu had lived at numerous places and with numerous relatives. She once lived in Ezeakuma(aka ochieogo) with her Uncle Ulu Orji she lived there while she attended basic primary school 1, at a point in time she briefly moved back in with her mother at Igbosha where she attended from primary 2 till was stopped and later returned to live with her uncle Ulu Orji the then eze ewe. It was during her time living with her uncle that she met her husband, got married and took on the name Enyidia Nwosu, she was married to Nwosu Okoro a bubbling young man then whose father and mother were from ugwuakuma and Isuochi respectively and equally late at the time, her father in-law known was known as Okoro Ukpai, in all this time as mama grew up she was said to be a very tenacious, intelligent and hardworking young lady evident in life and affairs. During the early days of her marriage was the eve of the Biafran civil war at a time when tensions were heightened and turned their marriage into a race for their life and survival. After her husband's return from conscription in the army they both settled down together living communally with their siblings, Mama is survived by 7 children plus an adoptive son between her and her husband, a co-wife, 3 step children, numerous cousins, relatives and friends of which her loss is extremely tragic for. Mama had always been known to be a hard working and kind woman despite going through sad losses, accidents and incidents that would bear down on any good soul she pushed forward and strived to make sure her children behaved exemplary and grew up well mannered, she had always been pragmatic when it comes to sensitive issues and had always loved education of which she could qualify as an educator for many of which through her experience she taught. Mama was always at the forefront pushing, supporting and even then going against the wishes of her husband that all of her children never skipped out of going to school was one of her biggest and most fervent goals although fate may have had its turn mama never gave up on aspiring for the wellbeing of all irrespective of her own interests. Mama despite her marriage was dearly loved and valued by all her half siblings and her only brother whom of which lived with mama during the early years of her marriage this love is what molded mama into being resilient in making sure anyone around her got along with each other she had always placed value on respecting one another and this precedent value promoted the bond and friendship she had with everyone. She was seen as hero or better still our dearly beloved MAMA
