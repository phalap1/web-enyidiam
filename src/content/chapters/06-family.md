Even after she married, her half-brothers and half-sisters loved her dearly and held her in high regard. So did her [only brother](#family-P005), who lived with her in the early years of her marriage.

That love shaped her. It made her determined that everyone around her should get along. She set great store by respect for one another, and that habit of respect is what built the friendships she had with so many people.

To us she was a hero. Better still, she was our dearly beloved Mama.
