For Primary 1 she lived at Ezeakuma, also called Ochieogo, with her uncle [Ulu Orji](#family-P004).

After that she went back for a while to her mother at Igbosha and went to school there from Primary 2, until her schooling was stopped. Later she returned to her uncle's house. By then Ulu Orji was the Eze Ewe.
