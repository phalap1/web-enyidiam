Mama passed away in 2026 after a brief illness, at the age of seventy-eight. Her husband, Mazi Nwosu Okoro, went before her.

She is survived by her seven children and an adopted son, her co-wife, three stepchildren, her grandchildren, and many cousins, relatives and friends. Her loss is felt deeply by every one of them.

*Mama, may your gentle soul rest in perfect peace. Amen.*
