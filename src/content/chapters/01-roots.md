Mama was born Mgbeke Kalu Okoro in 1948, a daughter of the Mgbocha family. Her father was [Kalu Okoro](#family-P002). Her mother, [Abenzi Kalu](#family-P003), came from Isuochi and married into Arochukwu.

As a girl she lived in more than one place and with more than one relative, and each of those homes had a hand in raising her.
