Mama was hard-working and kind, and she stayed that way through losses, accidents and troubles that would have worn down most people. She kept going. She made sure her children behaved well and grew up with good manners.

She was practical, most of all when something delicate had to be settled. And she loved education. She had the mind of a teacher, and a great many people learned from her experience.

Making sure that none of her children ever missed school was one of her deepest wishes. She pushed for it and stood behind it, even when it meant going against her husband's wishes. Life did not always go the way she hoped, but she never stopped working for the good of everyone around her, whatever it cost her.
