title: Marriage, and the name Enyidia
period: Before the war

It was while she was living with her uncle that she met [Nwosu Okoro](#family-P006), a lively young man at the time, and married him. With the marriage she took the name Enyidia Nwosu, the name most people came to know her by.

Her husband's father, [Okoro Ukpai](#family-P007), was from Ugwuakuma, and his mother was from Isuochi. Both had already passed on by then.

People who knew her as a young woman remember her as tenacious, bright and hard-working. Those same qualities showed in everything she did for the rest of her life.
