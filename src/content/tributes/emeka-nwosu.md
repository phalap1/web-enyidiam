She had a proverb for every problem and a pot of soup for every visitor. I never once left her house hungry or without advice.

When I failed my first exam she didn't scold me. She sat me down under the orange tree and told me about the years she walked to Arochukwu market before dawn, and said: "The one who falls and rises has not failed." I passed the next one. (placeholder tribute)
