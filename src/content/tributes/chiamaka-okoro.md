Every holiday at Obichie began the same way: Nne at the gate, wrapper tied tight, calling each of us by our full names as if she had been counting the days. She probably was.

She taught me to pound yam, to say my prayers in Igbo and to never leave a visitor standing. When I got my first job in Lagos she called every Sunday at exactly seven to ask if I had eaten. I will miss that call more than I can write.
