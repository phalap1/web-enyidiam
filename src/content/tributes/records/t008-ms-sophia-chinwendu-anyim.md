A TRIBUTE TO ENYIDIYA

I honestly do not know how to put into words what your passing means to me You were such a good mother kind hearted warm and loving I will always remember the beautiful person you were and the love and kindness you showed me Having you in my life was a blessing I will never take for granted.

I feel truly blessed to have known you to have been close to you and to have shared beautiful moments with you I will especially miss your prayers and the way you always wished and prayed for a better and beautiful life for me You always wanted to see me do well and that is something I will carry in my heart forever.

It hurts knowing that I will not get to see you again I wish we had more time but I find comfort in knowing that you lived a beautiful life and touched so many hearts along the way I am grateful for the time we shared and the memories you have left with me

I pray that the same God you always called upon in prayers for me will grant you eternal rest and comfort everyone you have left behind.

Thank you for your kindness your love your prayers and the beautiful memories You will forever have a special place in my heart.

Rest peacefully Enyidiya You are loved and you are deeply missed and will never be forgotten
