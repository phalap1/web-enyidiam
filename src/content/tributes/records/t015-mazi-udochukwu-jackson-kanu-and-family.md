TRIBUTE TO MY AUNT

Sadness struck our family when my last paternal aunt passed away. Our family grew to accept that her passing was imminent after a brief yet serious illness. But when she transitioned from this life to receive her heavenly reward, it was hard to grasp the reality of her passing. My life has been impacted by her untimely departure from this life to the next.

THINGS THAT I WILL MISS ABOUT MY AUNT

Some things were uniquely special about my Aunt that I will miss…

While no one else pronounced my son’s name the way my father would have done, she too had a unique way of saying his name. She simply called him “NDUKAKU” ( meaning life is greater than wealth).

Her angelic voice sends another message to the soul. She made most traditional marriages in ugwuakuma very fun and interesting wit her vocal sounds and coordinating gestures.. How she gorgeously takes the mgbede in search of her husband with a drink..

Kai!!!! Death has really stolen a whole lot from us…

You were like a mother I never had and up till now I will never believe u are gone. Even though I am absorbed in sadness as I reflect on specific things that made my relationship with my Aunt a cherished one, she embodied so much of which was her selfless act of giving and hospitality of which many can bear me witness…

Enyidiya gaa nke Oma till we meet on that resurrection morning..

Mazi. Udochukwu Jackson Kanu and family

Your Nephew
