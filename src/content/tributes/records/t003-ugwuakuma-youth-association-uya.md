TRIBUTE TO THE LATE MADAM MARGARET ENYIDIYA NWOSU

BY UGWUAKUMA YOUTH ASSOCIATION (UYA)

With deep sorrow and profound respect, the entire members of Ugwuakuma Youth Association (UYA) join the family, friends and well-wishers in honouring the life and legacy of Madam Margaret Enyidiya Nwosu.The news of your passing is a painful reminder that life is transient, but the memories and legacies we leave behind continue to live in the hearts of those we encounter.As a youth association, we recognise the importance of individuals whose lives and contributions form part of the history and development of our community. Your memory will remain a part of the story of our people, and we acknowledge this moment as an opportunity to celebrate a life that deserves to be remembered with honour and respect.Although words may not fully express the pain of your departure, we take comfort in the fact that a life remembered with love is never truly forgotten.To the bereaved family, we extend our heartfelt condolences. May God grant you the strength, courage and comfort to bear this great loss. May He also grant the departed eternal rest.

Rest peacefully, Madam Margaret Enyidiya Nwosu. You will be remembered with honour and respect.

Signed on behalf of Ugwuakuma Youth Association (UYA).

National PresidentUgwuakuma Youth Association (UYA)

Mazi John. O. Norbert

National SecretaryUgwuakuma Youth Association (UYA)
