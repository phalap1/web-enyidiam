In Loving Memory of Enyidia  On behalf of the Nwobi Family

Today, we honour the life of a remarkable woman whose love and strength shaped us all. Grandma was the heart of our family—her kindness, wisdom, and humour touched everyone she met.

Her final days were marked by unimaginable pain, yet she faced them with courage and grace, showing us what it means to fight until the end. Though her struggles are over, her legacy of love, resilience, and compassion will remain with us forever.

We will carry her memory in our stories, our traditions, and in the way we love one another. Her presence may be gone, but her spirit will continue to guide us.

Rest in peace, Enyidia.

You will always be loved and deeply missed.
