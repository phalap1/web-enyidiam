A TRIBUTE TO MY BELOVED MOTHER-IN-LAW

Losing Enyidiya leaves a quiet space in my life, and in our lives as a family, that can never truly be filled. Yet, even in our grief, our hearts remain filled with gratitude for the precious time we were blessed to share with her.

From the very beginning, Mama did more than welcome me into her family; she welcomed me into her heart. The day I married her son, I gained not just a mother-in-law, but a second mother. I will always be grateful for the remarkable woman she raised and the values she instilled in him. Mama made me feel at home and gave me a sense of belonging. She had a rare gift for making everyone around her feel seen, valued and loved. Through her strength, wisdom, humour and genuine concern for those she loved, she left an unforgettable mark on our lives.

You made loving you and bonding with you so effortless, Mama. I particularly grew fond of our weekend video calls. You would always notice my new hairstyles and take your time admiring my outfits, makeup and whatever else there was to admire. Those little moments meant so much to me. You were a woman of many stories, whether about history, politics or everyday happenings, and you never missed an opportunity to gently share your pearls of wisdom. I will miss those conversations more than words can express.

Mama was gentle yet fierce, compassionate, warm-hearted and exceptionally generous. Oh, how you loved to entertain and treat friends and family to delicious meals! You could wake up in the early hours of the morning just to prepare food and cater for a crowd, making sure everyone was well fed and welcomed. Your generosity knew no bounds, and your home was always a reflection of your beautiful heart.

Mama, you loved deeply, gave generously and lived meaningfully. You will be sorely missed!

Even in your moments of pain, you continued to bless us and give us hope. I find comfort in knowing that you are now resting with the angels, far away from sickness, stress and the discomforts of this earthly life. (Smiles through tears).

As difficult as this farewell is, we surrender to God’s will and, as Christians, accept His decision to call you home at this time. We pray for the grace to live lives worthy of His glory and to fulfil our own earthly missions until the day we are called home.

Rest well, Mama.

We are truly blessed to have known you, to have learnt from you and to have been part of your life’s journey. Your love, wisdom, kindness and generosity will remain with us, and we will continue to carry your memory forward.

May your gentle and strong soul rest peacefully, and may the beautiful legacy of love you have left behind continue to guide us.

Adieu, Mama. Until we meet again. Amen.

Irene Chinemeze Nwosu

(Nwannediya)
