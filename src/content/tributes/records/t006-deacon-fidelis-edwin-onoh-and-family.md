A TRIBUTE TO LATE MMA ENYIDIA NWOSU

Death remains one of life’s greatest mysteries and most painful realities. Yet, the passing of our beloved Mma Enyidia Nwosu in Enugu in March 2026 is one loss whose magnitude is difficult to comprehend.

Mma, your departure has created a colossal vacuum that will be difficult, if not impossible, to fill. You were a mother to all, a woman whose generosity knew no bounds and whose love transcended family ties. You embraced everyone as your own child, treating all with equal affection, kindness, and compassion. You knew no discrimination, and your home and heart were always open to all.

Your life was a shining example of selflessness, kindness, and motherly love. The many lives you touched and the memories you created will forever remain indelible in our hearts.

Mma, my heart is heavy, and words seem inadequate to express the depth of my grief. We would have wished to have you with us for many more years, but we take solace in the knowledge that God, in His infinite wisdom, knows best. As much as we wish you could stay, we acknowledge that God’s will is supreme, and His decisions are beyond human questioning.

Ngwanu, gaa nke ọma! May your gentle soul find eternal rest in the bosom of the Almighty God. Though your physical presence is no longer with us, your legacy of love, generosity, and compassion will continue to speak for you through the countless lives you touched.

Farewell, our dear Mma. You lived a life worthy of remembrance, and you will be sorely missed. Rest peacefully in the Lord until we meet again.

Adieu, a truly remarkable mother!

From the family of Deacon Fidelis Edwin Onoh.
