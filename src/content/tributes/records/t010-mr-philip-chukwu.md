Tribute to my lovely grandma

With a broken heart and equal surrender to the wish of the Maker I tearfully put down this tribute and farewell to my lovely Enyidia.

Mama I have chosen to pray and believe you are in a better place compared to what this life could offer, you have left lasting positive impact on many of which I consider myself lucky to be part of. For me it has never been easy coping with your loss because I can’t bear to imagine not having to hear you talk see you smiling or laughing anymore.

You have taught me many things of which consistency, goodwill and kindness is a major part of, you also made sure that none of us have ever been wrongfully hurt. You are my strongest inspiration for the application of wisdom in all things and you have recklessly and needlessly been good even when it seemed hopeless to be but now all this I look at and say Mama you are the best and thank you for teaching me, numerous things I have to say but I choose to only say this few.

Enyidia’m you will largely be missed because irrespective of how little it is I always had a share with you Mama’m a privilege I now realize is gone forever. It hurts!

Adieu Mama’m may the Good Lord grant you eternal peace and comfort in his bosom as we carry on your wonderful legacies.

Given the opportunity I will always choose to be your grandson.

Rest on Enyidia’m I LOVE YOU.

Mr Philip Chukwu Your Grandson
