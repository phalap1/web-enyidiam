To my beloved Grandma, Margaret Enyida Nwosu,

It is difficult to put into words just how much you meant to my siblings and I and how much you will be missed. Although I may not be physically present to say goodbye, please know that my heart is with you and the rest of our family today.

Grandma, you were such a kind, loving and generous woman. You had a special way of making everyone around you feel cared for, and those of us who were fortunate enough to know you will forever cherish the love you showed us.

I will always be grateful for the beautiful memories you gave my siblings and me. I remember our Christmas visits to the village, when you would always make sure we received our Christmas money. Those moments may have seemed like little gestures at the time, but they meant so much to us, and they are memories we will carry with us forever.

Even when it wasn’t Christmas, whenever you came to Abuja to visit and spend time with us, you never left without putting something in our hands. No matter how much Dad insisted that you shouldn’t, you always found a way to give us money. You simply wanted to see us happy and make sure we had the things we liked. That was just who you were: thoughtful, generous and always looking for ways to show your love.

You were also caring but firm, sweet but strict when you needed to be. You knew how to show love while making sure we understood the importance of respect and good upbringing. These are little parts of you that will remain with us and that we will always remember with a smile.

Grandma, thank you for every act of kindness, every visit, every gift, every word of advice and every beautiful memory. Thank you for the love you gave so freely, not just to us but to everyone fortunate enough to cross your path.

It hurts that we will no longer get to see you, hear your voice or experience your generosity in the same way, but I find comfort in knowing that the memories you created will never leave us. Your love lives on through your children, grandchildren and everyone whose life you touched.

Though we wish we had more time with you, we are grateful for the time we were blessed to share. You will always have a special place in our hearts, and you will never be forgotten.

Rest peacefully, our beloved Grandma. We love you, and we will miss you always.

May your beautiful soul rest in perfect peace.
