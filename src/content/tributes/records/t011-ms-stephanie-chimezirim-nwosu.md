\*\*A TRIBUTE TO MY BELOVED OGBO’M \*\*

Enyidiya, you were more than a grandmother to me. You were a blessing whose presence added something irreplaceable to our family. You represented a kind of love that cannot be measured, a warmth that made family feel like home, and a legacy that I pray will never be lost.

One of the greatest gifts you gave us was family. Through you, we have a story, roots, and generations connected by a bond that goes beyond blood. You reminded us, in your own beautiful way, that family is something to cherish, protect and hold close. And if there is one thing I will forever carry with me, it is the importance of loving my family deeply and never taking the blessing of having one for granted.

I will forever cherish the privilege of calling you my grandmother. I will miss the little things, the familiar voice, the warmth of your presence and the comfort that came with knowing you were here with us. It is difficult to accept that we must now learn to hold you in our memories rather than in our arms.

Ogbo’m, your life was a gift, and your legacy is one we must continue to honour. The love you poured into this family will not end with your departure. It will live through your children, grandchildren and generations yet unborn. It will live in the values we uphold, the traditions we preserve, the prayers we say and, most importantly, the way we continue to show up for one another.

I wish we had more time, more conversations, more moments and more opportunities to remind you just how much you are loved. But I am grateful for the time we shared and for the beautiful privilege of being one of the many lives your existence touched.

Thank you, Grandma, for the gift of family. Thank you for the roots you gave us and the love that will continue to bind us together. I pray that, as a family, we never lose the togetherness your life represents.

You may have departed from this world, but you will never depart from our hearts.

Rest peacefully, my beloved Ogbo’m.

Until we meet again,ogbo’m .

Yours truly,

Stephanie Chimezirim, Nwosu.
